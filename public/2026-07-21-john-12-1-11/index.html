Mary at the Feet of Jesus

July 21, 2026 · John 12:1-11
Luke 10:38-42; John 11:28-35

Across several moments in Mary's story, she is repeatedly found at the feet of Jesus—learning from Him, grieving before Him, and finally pouring out something extraordinarily costly in worship. Her story invites us to consider whether we are willing to keep returning to Jesus in every season: to learn, to grieve, and to give ourselves to Him in gratitude.
