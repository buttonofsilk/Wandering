What Is My Heart Meditating On?

November 19, 2026 · Isaiah 33:17-24

Isaiah moves from the promise of seeing the King in His beauty to a heart still remembering terror, and that tension feels remarkably human. We can know who God is and still be overwhelmed, grieving, anxious, or afraid. Being honest about what we are actually carrying does not mean staying there; it can become the moment when we receive help, remember who God is, and deliberately turn our focus back toward the One who is our Judge, Lawgiver, King, and Savior.
