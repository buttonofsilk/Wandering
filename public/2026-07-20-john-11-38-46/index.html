Alive, But Still Bound

July 20, 2026 · John 11:38-46
Ephesians 3:20

Lazarus walks out of the grave alive, but still wrapped in the burial cloths that had bound him. Reflecting on that image, Hope considers how Jesus calls us out of death into life—and how being made alive in Him does not mean we emerge instantly free from everything that once held us.
