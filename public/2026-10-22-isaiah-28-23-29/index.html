Tending the Garden of Our Minds

October 22, 2026 · Isaiah 28:23-29
Philippians 4:6-8; 1 Peter 5:7; 2 Corinthians 10:5; Psalm 42:5, 11; Psalm 77

Isaiah closes the chapter with the picture of a farmer who knows when to plow, how to plant, and how each crop should be harvested—a picture pointing us toward God's wisdom, justice, and sovereignty in how He deals with His people. That image also led us into other Scriptures that teach us how to tend the garden of our minds: acknowledging anxiety and despair, casting our cares on God, taking thoughts captive, remembering His faithfulness, and deliberately cultivating what is good and true.
