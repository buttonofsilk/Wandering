When God Hides His Eyes

September 2, 2026 · Isaiah 1:1-31
Psalm 80:3

Isaiah opens with people still bringing sacrifices, keeping festivals, and praying—yet God says He will hide His eyes from them because their hearts and actions are far from Him. The contrast with God making His face shine on His people made us pause over what He is really after: not simply doing the right things, but hearts that turn back toward Him.
