Isaiah 32 gives us glimpses of righteous rule, opened eyes and ears, peace, quietness, confidence, and secure dwelling places. We can build safeguards into our lives and use wisdom to protect what God has entrusted to us, but those things can quietly become substitutes for the security only He can give. The question is not whether we should use locks, cameras, or other protections, but whether our confidence has settled into them instead of into God.
