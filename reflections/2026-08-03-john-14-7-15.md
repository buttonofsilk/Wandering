In a passage filled with deep theology, it can be easy to feel pressure to understand and explain everything at once. Slowing down to simply observe what Jesus says reveals something foundational: He is in the Father, the Father is in Him, and His words and works carry the Father's authority—inviting us to let Scripture itself shape our understanding of who God is.
