Carrying forward the realization that Jesus entered His arrest with full authority and purpose, this reflection looks at His words before Annas in a different light—not as defensiveness, but as a reminder that He had never hidden who He was. Following Jesus' words back into Isaiah and then forward to Caiaphas' earlier prophecy, the reflection traces the way Jesus openly fulfills what had already been spoken about Him.
