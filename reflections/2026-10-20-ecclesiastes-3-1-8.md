Today we pause our journey through Isaiah to mourn the loss of a man whose life reflected a deep love for Jesus even through tremendous difficulty. Ecclesiastes reminds us that there is a time to weep, mourn, laugh, and dance, and today we make room for both grief and the hope of heaven. His life also leaves us with a question for our own: Are we loving people well, being present, and faithfully planting the seeds God asks us to plant?
