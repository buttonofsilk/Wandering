As Grandma nears the end of her life, Psalm 100 gives us somewhere to turn our eyes: toward thanksgiving, praise, and the goodness of God. Grief does not erase what God has done or make Him less worthy; even when life does not unfold the way we wanted, we can thank Him for the lives we have been given, remember the preciousness of our days, and learn that He is trustworthy even in the hardest places.
