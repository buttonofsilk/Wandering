As Jesus faces what is coming, His soul is troubled—but He immediately brings that trouble to His Father and chooses the purpose He came to fulfill. This reflection considers the difference between having a thought, feeling, or temptation and what we choose to do with it, encouraging us to bring those moments to God rather than allowing them to take root and lead us somewhere they should not.
