God tells Ahaz to ask for a sign, and Ahaz refuses—something that can sound faithful until Isaiah's response tells us otherwise. It brought Gideon and his fleece to mind and made us wonder about the heart behind asking God for confirmation. Sometimes asking can come from unbelief, but could it also come from faith—from a willingness to move forward while wanting to be sure we are following God correctly?
