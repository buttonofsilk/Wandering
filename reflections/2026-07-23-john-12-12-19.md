John gives a remarkably simple account of Jesus' triumphal entry, drawing attention to fulfilled Scripture and to the testimony spreading after Lazarus was raised from the dead. Rather than rushing ahead to what comes next, this reflection pauses with John's continuing message: this is who Jesus is, and everything is unfolding according to God's timing.
