Reading Isaiah 7 left us with questions about Ahaz that sent us backward into 2 Kings 16 for more of the story. Isaiah tells Ahaz to trust God, but Kings shows us where Ahaz actually turns: he calls himself the servant and son of the king of Assyria and sends him silver and gold from the house of the Lord. Scripture itself gives us the context that changes how we understand what we read.
