Jesus repeatedly invites His disciples to remain in Him, using the image of branches connected to a vine to show where life and fruitfulness come from. This reflection considers what it means to truly remain connected to Jesus—not merely to be among the branches—and the promise that as we abide in Him, He also makes His dwelling with us.
