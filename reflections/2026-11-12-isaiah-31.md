After chapter upon chapter of warnings, it can become easy to hear another one and stop listening—just as repeated warnings can lose their urgency when the expected danger seems slow to arrive. But Isaiah 31 brings us to a remarkably simple invitation for people described as profoundly obstinate: return to God. Our stubbornness, pride, sin, or shame do not make Christ's work insufficient; the question is whether we will humble ourselves, repent, and return to the One who is calling us back.
