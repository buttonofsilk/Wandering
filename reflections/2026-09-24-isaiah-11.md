Isaiah keeps returning to promises, judgment, restoration, and what God will do, adding a little more detail each time. What can feel repetitive began to look more like something being built layer by layer—promises Isaiah's first hearers could only wait for, while we can now look back and see some of them fulfilled in Jesus. That gives us something to hold onto while we wait for promises and answers we cannot see yet.
