Isaiah 13 does not give us much room to escape the uncomfortable language of God's judgment, and sometimes our instinct is to do exactly that—to make Scripture harsher by adding our own rules or softer by padding its sharper edges. But there is another choice: let God's Word say what it says and stay in the uncomfortable place long enough for the Holy Spirit to work in our hearts and bring us into alignment with Him.
