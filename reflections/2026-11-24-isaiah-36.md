After spending chapter after chapter hearing Isaiah warn Judah about Assyria, Isaiah 36 suddenly puts names and faces to those prophecies: Sennacherib is attacking, Shebna is no longer over the household, Eliakim has taken his place, and an Assyrian official is openly trying to undermine Jerusalem’s trust in God. Watching earlier words become present reality gives weight to the promises still awaiting fulfillment. The God who spoke faithfully then remains trustworthy now.
