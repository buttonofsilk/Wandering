Jerusalem saw its vulnerabilities and responded with weapons, water, walls, and careful preparation—but Isaiah exposes what was missing: they did not look to the One who had made and planned it. Preparation itself was not the problem; dependence on their own preparation was. Their self-sufficiency invites us to examine the things we use to make ourselves feel secure and ask whether we are ultimately trusting what our hands can accomplish or the God who holds it all.
