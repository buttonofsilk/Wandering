Hezekiah is living with consequences that began long before him. Ahaz chose his own solution instead of trusting God, and decades later Hezekiah faces Assyria at Jerusalem’s doorstep. He cannot undo the choice that helped set this in motion, but he can choose what he does now—and his first response is to humble himself and turn to God. We cannot control every decision that affects us, but we are still responsible for what we do with what has been handed to us.
