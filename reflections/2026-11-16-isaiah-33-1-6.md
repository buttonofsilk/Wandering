Isaiah's prayer begins with people waiting for the Lord, but Israel's story reminds us that waiting on God has often been mixed with attempts to secure our own solutions. Isaiah 33 gives us the reason we can keep returning to Him even when we wait imperfectly: the Lord Himself is the stability of our times. When everything around us feels uncertain, the question becomes whether we will keep trying to solve things our own way or learn to wait for Him.
