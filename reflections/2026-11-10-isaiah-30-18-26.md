After so much warning and judgment, Isaiah 30 pauses over the gracious heart of God: He longs to be gracious, has compassion, and blesses those who long for Him. That word opens a trail through Isaiah—from seeking God, to longing for Him, to waiting eagerly for Him, and finally to trusting His promise to lead us along paths we have not known. The question becomes deeply personal: Is time with God something we simply schedule, or has knowing Him become something we genuinely long for?
