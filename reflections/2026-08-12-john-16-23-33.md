This reflection follows a trail about peace—what it is, what it is not, and where it actually comes from. Rather than promising a life without trouble, Scripture repeatedly points us back to God: bringing our worries to Him, guarding what we allow our minds to dwell on, and learning to keep our focus on Jesus in the middle of a world that still brings tribulation.
