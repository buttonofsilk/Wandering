God sends Assyria against Israel in judgment, but then Isaiah tells us something important: Assyria has intentions of its own. Its king boasts about what he accomplished by his own power and wisdom, and God holds him accountable for his arrogance. The difference made us stop over God's sovereignty, human motives, and why being used within something God is doing does not make every human intention righteous.
