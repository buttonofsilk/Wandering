In the middle of judgment, war, and a people repeatedly turning away from God, Isaiah suddenly gives us this beautiful picture of hope—a Child who will be called Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God, Eternal Father, Prince of Peace. They could only look forward to a promise they did not yet fully understand. We get to look back and know His name.
