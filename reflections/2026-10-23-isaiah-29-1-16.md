Familiar verses can make it easy to move quickly toward what we already understand, but slowing down in Isaiah 29 brought the surrounding warning into view. God confronts people who honor Him with their lips while their hearts remain far away, and Jesus later uses Isaiah's words while confronting human tradition that invalidates God's Word. The passage leaves us asking whether we are truly allowing God to shape our understanding—or turning things around and placing ourselves, our teachers, or our traditions above the One who formed us.
