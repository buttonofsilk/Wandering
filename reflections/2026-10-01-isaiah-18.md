Isaiah 18 drops us into another nation and another complicated moment in history, but this time the picture that caught our attention was surprisingly quiet. While nations move and earthly powers struggle around Him, God says He will quietly look from His dwelling place—and people outside Israel are turning toward Him. When our own lives feel anything but quiet, there is something beautiful about remembering that God is not ruffled by any of it.
