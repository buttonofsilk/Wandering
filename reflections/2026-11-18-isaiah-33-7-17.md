Isaiah asks a startling question: Who can dwell with the devouring fire and everlasting burning of God's holiness? The answer is not that we manufacture enough righteous behavior to make ourselves safe, but that our lives are transformed as we abide in God, surrender to His work, and learn to walk in what He produces in us. His holiness remains consuming, but for those brought into alignment with Him, Isaiah also speaks of refuge, provision, and security.
