Isaiah describes the voice of the Lord coming against Assyria with terrifying authority, while Psalm 29 fills out that picture with a voice powerful enough to thunder over the waters, break cedars, and shake the wilderness. But Jesus gives us another side of that same voice: His sheep listen, know it, and follow. The difference is not in the power of the One speaking, but in our relationship with Him—and knowing His voice requires continuing to listen.
