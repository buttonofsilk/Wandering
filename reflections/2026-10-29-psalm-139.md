Psalm 139 once became a place of comfort during anxious nights, and years later its words return in another hospital room while sitting beside Grandma. God knows our thoughts, surrounds us, formed us, and invites us to let Him search what still needs to change. Instead of only asking what we were created to do, perhaps we also need to ask who God created us to be—and learn to abide, rest, trust, and allow Him to shape us into that person.
