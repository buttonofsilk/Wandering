After the warnings and discipline earlier in Isaiah 29 comes a picture of remarkable reversal: the deaf hear, the blind see, the afflicted find joy, evil comes to an end, and those who err come to understand. God's correction is not like imperfect human discipline; He is just, righteous, loving, and kind. There is hope even when our thinking is wrong—if we are willing to receive His correction and come into alignment with Him.
