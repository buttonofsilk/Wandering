Isaiah 9 says the people would not turn back to God who struck them, but Isaiah 10:20 describes something different: the remnant will no longer rely on Assyria, the one who struck them, and will truly rely on the Lord. That difference in both the person and the word matters. It made us stop over what we rely on for comfort, help, and peace—and whether what feels like peace is actually God's peace.
