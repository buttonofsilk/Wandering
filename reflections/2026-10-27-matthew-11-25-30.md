After another day at the hospital, Jesus' invitation to the weary and burdened felt especially close: "Come to Me." He does not reprimand us for being tired or demand that we become strong enough first; He invites us to come, learn from Him, and find rest for our souls. Some days make our need for His strength easier to recognize, but the invitation is just as true in an ordinary day as it is in a difficult one.
