Sometimes we come to Scripture ready to seek God, study carefully, and listen—and nothing seems to jump off the page. Isaiah 23 reminded us that we do not need to manufacture a personal application simply to make our reading feel productive. Even when a passage gives us history more than an immediate connection to our own lives, it is still God's Word, and we can sit with it, wrestle with it, and continue seeking Him.
