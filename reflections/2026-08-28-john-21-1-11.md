After everything they've been through, the disciples go fishing—and Jesus meets them there. We get Peter throwing himself into the water to get to Him, John quietly noting that everyone else used the boat, and Jesus already waiting on shore with breakfast. There's something wonderfully human here about being known so well by Jesus and still being invited deeper into relationship with Him.
