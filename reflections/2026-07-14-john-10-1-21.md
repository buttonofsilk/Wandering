Jesus describes Himself as the Good Shepherd whose sheep know and follow His voice. Reflecting on David's willingness to pursue and rescue his sheep, Hope considers both the Shepherd who faithfully comes after us and the kind of sheep she wants to become—one who recognizes His voice and eagerly follows rather than continually wandering away.
