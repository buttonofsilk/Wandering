Isaiah's judgment against Damascus and Ephraim points back to something deeper than the alliances and battles surrounding them: they had forgotten the God of their salvation and mixed their worship of Him with other gods. That made us stop over the quieter ways divided allegiance can happen in our own lives. Following Jesus means a change of master—not fitting Him alongside whatever else we would rather keep.
