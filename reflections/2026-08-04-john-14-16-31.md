Jesus continues to unfold the relationship between Himself, the Father, and the Holy Spirit, promising His disciples that He will not leave them as orphans. Rather than rushing through such a rich passage, this reflection sits with Jesus' repeated connection between loving Him and following His words—and the remarkable promise that God will make His dwelling with those who love Him.
