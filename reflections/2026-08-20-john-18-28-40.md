Surrounded by political tension and people trying to decide what to do with Him, Jesus remains completely clear about who He is and the kingdom He came to establish. Following Pilate's questions about kingship and truth, this reflection turns the question back toward us: does the Jesus we believe in actually line up with the Jesus who reveals Himself in Scripture?
