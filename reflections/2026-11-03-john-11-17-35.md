On the day Grandma left this world, two words from John 11 feel especially precious: "Jesus wept." Jesus met Martha in her questions and Mary in her grief, and He does not require us to hide anger, hurt, or tears from Him. As we mourn those we love, we can bring the fullness of our grief to the One who knows what it is to weep—while still remembering who He is and keeping our eyes on Him.
