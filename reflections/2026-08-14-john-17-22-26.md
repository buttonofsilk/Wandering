This reflection wrestles with the astonishing idea that some of what Jesus accomplished for us is not only a future promise—it has already been given. Sitting with Jesus' words about glory and Paul's words in Romans, it considers what it means to spend a lifetime learning to live in the victory, love, and new identity Christ has already made possible, rather than continually trying to earn what He has given.
