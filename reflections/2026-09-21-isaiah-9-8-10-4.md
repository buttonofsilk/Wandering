Isaiah repeatedly says that God's hand is still stretched out and that the people did not turn back to the One who struck them. For someone whose experience of human authority has included abuse, control, or manipulation, those words can be difficult to hear without bringing human experiences into how we understand God. This passage made us stop and ask what changes when we begin with who God actually is.
