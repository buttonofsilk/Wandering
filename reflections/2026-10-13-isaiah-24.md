Isaiah 24 widens the view from judgment against individual nations to judgment encompassing the earth, while still leaving us glimpses of people raising their voices to glorify God. Its reference to a broken "everlasting covenant" sent us back to Genesis and raised difficult questions about the value of human life, faithfulness to God, and whether we recognize warnings when they come. Through judgment, a remnant's praise, and Isaiah's anguish, God remains sovereign and His purposes continue. *Context note: Genesis 9:5 is speaking specifically about God requiring an accounting for human lifeblood and reflects the sanctity of human life.
