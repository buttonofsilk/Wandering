Stepping away from the usual reflection, this recording explains the SOAP method—Scripture, Observation, Application, and Prayer—and how it can help us slow down and listen for what God is showing us in His Word. The goal isn't a perfect study process, but simply to spend time with God, invite the Holy Spirit into the reading, and allow Scripture to work its way into everyday life.
