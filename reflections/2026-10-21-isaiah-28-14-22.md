Isaiah's warning to Jerusalem confronts something uncomfortable: we can become so settled inside falsehood and deception that we no longer recognize them for what they are. But God—not us—sets the measuring line of justice and the level of righteousness, and what we have built for ourselves cannot change His standard. Even within this severe warning, God points toward a firmly placed foundation and invites us to consider where we have actually placed our trust.
