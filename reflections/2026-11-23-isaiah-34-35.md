Isaiah 34 gives us a severe picture of God’s judgment, and Isaiah 35 answers it with restoration: barren places bloom, weak hands are strengthened, fearful hearts are told to take courage, blind eyes are opened, deaf ears hear, and sorrow finally gives way to joy. God’s holiness and judgment are not easy to sit with, but neither are they the end of the story for those who belong to Him. Even in seasons of exhaustion and grief, His promise points us toward a day when sorrow and sighing will flee away.
