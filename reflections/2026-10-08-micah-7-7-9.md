After days in difficult passages, we stepped away from Isaiah for a moment to focus simply on God—and found ourselves back with the image of a watchman waiting through the night. Micah and the Psalms reminded us that while we wait and seek God, we still choose where to place the limited emotional energy we have. We can spend it feeding anger and frustration, or turn toward prayer, God's goodness, and loving the people He has placed in front of us.
