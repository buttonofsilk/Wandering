This reflection follows the disciples as they try to understand a future that suddenly looks very different from what they expected—and connects that uncertainty to the moments when our own lives take an unexpected turn. It considers how God often gives us enough to take the next step rather than revealing the entire plan, using the waiting and uncertainty to shape who we are becoming while asking us to keep trusting Him.
