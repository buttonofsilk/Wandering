After speaking to His disciples about abiding, love, and friendship, Jesus changes the tone and prepares them for what following Him will also cost. He tells them plainly that the world that rejected and persecuted Him may reject them too, while reminding them that He chose them out of the world and that the Holy Spirit would help them testify about Him.
