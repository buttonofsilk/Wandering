When Hezekiah’s servants arrive, Isaiah seems ready with an answer from God. His relationship with the Lord was not something he suddenly reached for because Jerusalem had entered a crisis; he had spent decades listening, praying, and faithfully walking with Him. Hard seasons can expose how easily other things steal our attention, but they can also remind us why we need to cultivate our relationship with God before the crisis comes.
