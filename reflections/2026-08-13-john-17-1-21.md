This reflection sits with the depth of Jesus' prayer and the things about Him that can feel almost too large to fully comprehend—from His existence before the world began to His relationship with the Father. Then comes an astonishingly personal realization: Jesus' prayer reaches beyond the disciples sitting with Him that night to those who would one day believe through their testimony—including us.
