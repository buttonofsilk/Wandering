Isaiah 28 returns us to judgment, warning a people who would not listen even when God offered them rest. Psalm 77 gives us a very different response in the middle of distress: Asaph cries out honestly, asks painful questions, and then deliberately turns his attention toward remembering God's works and faithfulness. When life is hard and God is speaking, we have a choice about where we turn our attention: Will we refuse to listen, or will we look up, remember, and hear Him?
