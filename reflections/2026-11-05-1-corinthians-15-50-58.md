Paul takes words that appear within the severity of judgment in Hosea and uses them in 1 Corinthians 15 as a taunt against a defeated enemy: death does not get the final word. After being confronted with the preciousness and frailty of life, we are reminded of the extraordinary hope of resurrection through Jesus Christ. That hope leaves us with a question: Do we value it enough to boldly share the truth with those around us?
