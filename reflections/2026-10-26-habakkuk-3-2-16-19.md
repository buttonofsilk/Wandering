Sometimes one phone call is enough to bring everything else in life to a stop. Habakkuk does not hide fear, trembling, or distress, but neither does he remain there: even while facing what may come, he turns his eyes toward God and chooses to rejoice in the God of his salvation. We can be honest about fear, grief, worry, and pain while still learning to say, "Even if," and turning toward the One we trust.
