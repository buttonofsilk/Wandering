After struggling through the repeated judgment of Isaiah, chapter 26 gives us a beautiful place to sit with trust, peace, longing, discipline, and promise. Scripture will not always be easy, and we will not always immediately understand what is different or what God is showing us—but we can keep seeking Him. When Scripture is hard, when life is hard, and when every other voice grows loud, we can return our attention to God and let His voice become louder.
