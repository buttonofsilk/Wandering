Isaiah moves through Babylon, Assyria, Philistia, and Moab, sometimes looking further into the future and sometimes closer to Isaiah's own time. Kingdoms rise, pride is brought low, judgment comes, and promises remain—but God never loses control of the story. Through all of it, we keep seeing the same God: sovereign, just, righteous, compassionate, and completely unsurprised by anything happening on the map.

*Proverbs 21:1 — "The king's heart is like channels of water in the hand of the Lord; He turns it wherever He wishes."
