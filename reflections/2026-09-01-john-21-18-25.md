Peter looks at John and asks, essentially, "But what about him?" Jesus' answer is wonderfully direct: "What is that to you? You follow Me." Then John gives us another caution—don't make Jesus' words say more than He actually said. Together, they leave us watching our own obedience and handling Scripture carefully rather than getting distracted by someone else's path.
