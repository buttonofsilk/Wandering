Even at the cross, I kept seeing the same thing that has followed Jesus from Gethsemane onward: He is still fully in authority, and He is still completely surrendered to the Father. He pauses to care for His mother, says, "It is finished," and then gives up His spirit—a Savior who was not overcome by what was happening to Him, but faithfully chose to walk all the way through it.
