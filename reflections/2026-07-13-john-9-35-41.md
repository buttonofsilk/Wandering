Jesus' words to the Pharisees raise a challenging question: are we teachable, or are we so certain of what we already believe that we cannot recognize truth when God puts it in front of us? This reflection considers what it looks like to invite the Holy Spirit into both Scripture and our circumstances, allowing Him to reveal what we may not yet see in our own hearts.
