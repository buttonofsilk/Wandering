Martha and Mary come to Jesus carrying the same loss, but He responds to each of them differently—meeting Martha with truth and hope, and Mary with compassion and tears. Even knowing that Lazarus would soon walk out of the grave, Jesus entered into their grief and met each sister exactly where she was.
