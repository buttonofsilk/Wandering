Continuing the thread from Gethsemane and Jesus' arrest, this reflection sits with the contrast between how powerless Jesus appears and the authority He still possesses. Even while mocked, beaten, and moved steadily toward the cross, Jesus makes clear that Pilate's authority exists only because it has been given from above—and He continues willingly toward the sacrifice He came to make.
