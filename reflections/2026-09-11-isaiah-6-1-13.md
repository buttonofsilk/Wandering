Isaiah sees God on His throne, the train of His robe filling the temple, while the seraphim cry "Holy, holy, holy" loudly enough to shake the thresholds. Faced with God's holiness, Isaiah recognizes his own uncleanness, is cleansed, and then responds to God's call before knowing the incredibly difficult assignment that is coming: "Here I am. Send me." And even after the devastation God describes, one small line remains—the holy seed is its stump.
