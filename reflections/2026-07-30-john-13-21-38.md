Some days in Scripture bring an immediate insight, and some days simply mean reading, noticing, and staying present with God's Word. Walking through Judas' betrayal, Jesus' command to love one another, and Peter's coming denial, this reflection is a reminder that time in Scripture is still worthwhile even when there isn't one clear application or lesson that jumps off the page.
