Isaiah's vineyard begins with careful preparation: the stones are removed, choice vines are planted, a watchtower is built, and even the wine vat is ready. God prepared it expecting good grapes, yet what grew was worthless. That made us wonder about what God prepares and places before us—and whether we trust Him enough to step into it obediently even when we cannot see what He intends to do with it.
