In Isaiah 27, God describes Himself as the keeper of His vineyard—the One who waters it continually and guards it day and night. But tucked into the imagery of briars, thorns, protection, and peace is a little word that made us stop: "or." Isaiah's words reminded us that opposition is not our only option; we can rely on God's protection and make peace with Him, and that choice led us to consider the places in our own lives where we have forgotten that we can choose a different way.
