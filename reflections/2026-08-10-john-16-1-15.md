title: When You Cannot Bear It Yet
scripture: John 16:1-15
themes: [Holy Spirit, Persecution]
audio: 2026-08-10.mp3

Jesus prepares His disciples for hardship with compassion, promising that although He is leaving, the Holy Spirit will come to guide, teach, and remain with them. He gives them enough to hold onto now, knowing they cannot bear everything at once, and reminds them that remembering His words will help steady them when hard things come.
