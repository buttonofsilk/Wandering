Isaiah's harsh vision made us stop over an assumption that can quietly shape discernment: that something from God should feel peaceful, comforting, or reassuring to us. But Scripture does not always fit the emotional categories we create for it, and neither does the way God speaks. Isaiah's anguish challenges us to bring what we hear back to God for discernment rather than accepting or dismissing it simply because of how it makes us feel.
