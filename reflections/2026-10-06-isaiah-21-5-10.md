We returned to Isaiah 21 because God still had more for us in the same passage. Israel had not yet experienced the Babylonian exile, yet God was already speaking of Babylon's eventual fall—a promise whose fulfillment stood beyond suffering they had not even begun to experience. That reminds us that painful circumstances, unanswered prayers, and outcomes different from what we hoped do not change who God is: He is still sovereign, still Lord, and still faithful.
