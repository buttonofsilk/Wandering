Isaiah 30 warns people who make their own plans, seek security somewhere other than God, and would rather hear pleasant words than uncomfortable truth. It can be easy to turn a passage like this outward and identify where everyone else is getting it wrong, but the harder question is personal: Where am I making plans without God, accepting comfortable illusions, or looking somewhere else for refuge? His invitation brings us back to repentance, rest, quietness, and trust.
