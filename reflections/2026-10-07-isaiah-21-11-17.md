After several days of asking what happens when following God is hard, Isaiah 21 brings us to another question: What do we do while we are still in the middle of it? The watchman says morning is coming, but night is still here, while fugitives fleeing battle receive water and bread along the way. We may not know the outcome yet, but we can keep inquiring, keep seeking God, notice His provision, and trust Him here in the middle.
