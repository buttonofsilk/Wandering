## Hold these together

Scripture is technicolor, where we sometimes want to think in monotone.

@pairs

Acts 2:37-40 | Romans 10:8-13

Ezekiel 18:21-32 | 1 Timothy 2:3-6

Joshua 24:14-24 | Romans 6:15-23

Jeremiah 31:31-34 | Ezekiel 36:25-27

@end

What becomes clearer—or more complex—when you let these passages stand beside one another?
